Quick test protocol

  1. Mount phone and sample. Keep LED position, distance and angle fixed.
  2. Tap Start camera, then drag the ROI onto one uniform coloured area.
  3. Tap Start analysis. Test 1, 2, 5 and 10 Hz separately for 10 s each. For a pulse-like signal, read the result in bpm.
  4. Export the CSV and labelled Hue–time PNG plot for later calibration.

Research prototype: browser camera settings may vary by iPhone model. Use the same phone and fixed illumination for comparisons.
